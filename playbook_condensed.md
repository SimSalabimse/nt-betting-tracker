**Staking Logic**: Individual per-bet (higher stake for clearest +EV/low-variance; lower for marginal). Flat only when many strong uncorrelated leans. Explicit EV + expected P/L = stake × EV in every rec. 10-20 NOK cap.

**Bankroll & Tracking**: Log every bet in bet_log.csv (clean CSV). Update current_bankroll.md additively after settlements. Track ROI per sport/league/market. Strict daily loss cap. Reset protocols if hit.
